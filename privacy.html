Steply privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Steply is a visual routine app for parents and other grown-ups who care for a child. A grown-up builds picture routines and a child follows them one card at a time. This version of Steply has no accounts and no cloud.

The short version

  • Your routines, photos, voice recordings and your child's progress are stored in Steply's storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to us.
  • The only thing that reaches us is a problem report, and only if you choose to send one.
  • Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud), printing and exporting are under your control; see below.
  • No account, no ads, no selling of data, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your child's first name or nickname; routines, steps, times, Plan B cards and reminders; photos you take or pick for a step (saved without their location and camera data) and voice recordings you make; the record of which steps were done, stars and rewards; your settings and PIN.

Spoken step labels use your phone's own speech engine. Reminders are notifications created on the phone. Steply does not ask for your child's age, birth date, diagnosis, school, location or contacts, and has no advertising identifier.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose it:

  • Problem report (Settings > Support). You see the full text before you tap Send. It contains what you type (a name you choose and what happened) and a short diagnostic (app version, device model, OS version, recent error codes) with personal content removed; no photos. It goes to our server in Frankfurt, Germany (Supabase, on Amazon Web Services; Supabase is a US company), and we keep it 30 days. To prevent abuse we keep a salted hash of your IP address for about one hour. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in fixing Steply (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Exporting a routine creates a file protected by a passphrase you choose; you decide where it goes.
  • Printing uses your phone's print service.
  • Your phone's backup. On iOS, your iCloud backup may include Steply's data, under Apple's terms. On Android, Steply's data is excluded from Google's cloud backup.
  • App stores. Apple and Google deliver the app and handle any purchase under their own privacy policies.

Children

Steply is used by parents for their children. A child uses it only in Child mode, which has no sign-up, links, purchases, ads or settings and never asks the child for information. Nothing about your child is sent to us. If a problem report you send mentions a child, we delete it after 30 days, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights and choices

Everything is on your phone: you can see, change and delete it in the app, or delete all of it by deleting the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. For a problem report you sent, or any question, write to support@nisico.eu; we answer within one month. EU and UK users can complain to their data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).
