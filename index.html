How it works

From a hard morning to “Done!” in three steps

Pick a ready-made routine, make it yours, and hand over the screen. Steply does the showing, so you can do the helping.

Pick a routine

Start from ready-made routines for mornings, bedtime, school or outings. Swap in photos of your child's own things.

See the day

Today shows what is next in one big card, and the rest of the day below it. Tap Hand to your child.

One step at a time

Your child sees one big picture and one Done button. A parent lock keeps everything else closed.

Help now

When the day goes off plan

Rain, a closed park, a meltdown at the door. One tap on Help now, and your child sees something calm and clear, full screen.

  • Breathe together. A slow circle grows and shrinks: breathe in, rest, breathe out. Quiet: no sound and no vibration.
  • First / Then. Two pictures in seconds: first shoes, then tablet.
  • Change of plan. Swap a routine or reset the rest of today. Your usual routines are kept for tomorrow.

What Steply does

Picture routines for the whole day

Questions

Parents ask us

Who is Steply for?

Parents of autistic children ages 3 to 7, starting with routines at home. No diagnosis is needed: any family that uses picture schedules can use it, and grown-ups can use it for their own day too.

What does my child see?

In child mode your child sees one big picture step at a time and one Done button, with the steps ahead shown as small dots. A parent lock keeps everything else closed. At the end there is a calm All done screen.

What happens when the day goes off plan?

Tap Help now. Breathe together shows a slow, calm circle to breathe with. First / Then shows two pictures in seconds. Change of plan lets you swap a routine or reset the rest of today, and your usual routines are kept.

Does my child need an account or their own tablet?

No. Steply works on the phone or tablet you already have, iPhone, iPad or Android. You do not need an account to start.

Is my child's data private?

Your child never needs an account. Routines and photos you add stay on your device unless you choose to back them up or share a link. There are no ads and we never sell data.

How much does it cost?

Steply is free to download and everyday routines are free, with no ads. Steply Plus is an optional subscription with extras such as week and month planning. You see the price in the App Store or Google Play before you pay, and we email you 2 days before any trial turns into a charge.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Start from ready-made routines with clear 3D pictures, then swap any picture for a photo of your child's own toothbrush, backpack or bed.

Can I print the pictures?

Yes. You can print picture cards and boards from the app, and there are free printable boards on this site for the fridge or a day without screens.

Is Steply a therapy?

No. Steply is a visual support and planning tool. It does not diagnose or treat anything and does not replace your child's doctor, therapist or teacher. Many families use it alongside the plan they already follow.
